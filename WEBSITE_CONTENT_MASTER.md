Across Maharashtra, HR and Industrial Relations professionals handle complex legal compliance and diverse workforces. Despite their critical contribution, there was historically no dedicated state-level platform bringing Marathi and Maharashtrian HR professionals together under one institutional umbrella.

**MAHA HR FORUM** was founded by **Shivram Thavare** to address this void. Registered as a not-for-profit company under **Section 8 of the Companies Act, 2013**, the Forum serves as an independent, non-political body committed strictly to professional advancement, statutory literacy, and industrial harmony.
