About The Forum

A Dedicated Institutional Umbrella for Maharashtra’s Human Capital

Across Maharashtra’s industrial corridors—from Pune, Chakan, Aurangabad, and Kolhapur to Mumbai and Nagpur—thousands of dedicated professionals drive India's human capital.

MAHA HR FORUM is registered under Section 8 of the Companies Act, 2013 as an autonomous, not-for-profit professional body created to unite, support, and elevate our HR community. It serves as an independent, non-political body committed strictly to professional advancement, statutory literacy, and industrial harmony.

Applied mastery of Factory Laws, EPF, ESIC, and the 4 New Labour Codes.

Industrial Harmony

Strengthening shopfloor dialogue, collective bargaining, and bilateral peace.

Professional Framework

Core Areas of Action

A comprehensive institutional framework designed to empower HR professionals from the factory shopfloor to the executive boardroom.

01

Statutory Mastery & New Labour Codes

Practical workshops and compliance calendars covering Factory Laws, EPF, ESIC, CLRA, and the 4 New Labour Codes.

02

Industrial Relations & Workplace Harmony

Proactive shopfloor dialogue, collective bargaining best practices, and peaceful dispute resolution across MIDCs.

03

Digital HR & AI Transformation

Preparing HR teams for the future of work with actionable training in HR Analytics, Generative AI, and HRIS automation.

04

Youth Employability & Apprenticeships

Connecting students and fresh graduates (MSW, MBA, Law) with mentors, CV clinics, and industrial placement drives.

05

Dignity, POSH & Workplace Inclusion

Training Internal Committees (IC), promoting gender equity, workplace safety, and compliance with the POSH Act, 2013.

06

MSME & Plant-Level Capacity Building

Practical SOPs, audit readiness checklists, and supervisory development designed for small and medium manufacturing enterprises.

Leadership Perspective
“Maharashtra has always led India’s industrial journey. Behind every productive plant and successful enterprise is the untiring effort of our Human Resource professionals. Maha HR Forum exists to ensure that every practitioner—from shopfloor IR officers in remote MIDC belts to corporate leaders in Mumbai—has a trusted community to learn, lead, and grow together.”

A seasoned HR leader with extensive experience across Maharashtra’s manufacturing, industrial, and corporate sectors. Recognizing the unique challenges faced by HR professionals on the shopfloor and in corporate boardrooms, he established Maha HR Forum to create a supportive, permanent institutional ecosystem that equips practitioners with knowledge, solidarity, and voice.
