Statutory Foundation

Incorporated under Section 8 of the Companies Act, 2013

MAHA HR FORUM is incorporated as an autonomous, not-for-profit professional company under Section 8 of the Companies Act, 2013 (Ministry of Corporate Affairs, Government of India). Our legal charter is dedicated exclusively to promoting education, applied research, professional training, statutory compliance, industrial harmony, and social welfare in Human Resource Management and Industrial Relations.

Central Statutory Purpose: To advance human resource management, industrial relations, and labour welfare as essential pillars of Maharashtra’s industrial growth, operating strictly without any commercial profit motive or dividend distribution.

For complete institutional clarity and legal transparency, our charter is presented below in two complementary structures: first, as 8 Thematic Strategic Domains designed for practical understanding; followed by the full, verbatim text of all 30 Official MoA Charter Clauses as approved by the Registrar of Companies (RoC).

Strategic Framework

8 Thematic Focus Domains

Our 30 statutory MoA clauses synthesize into eight strategic pillars designed to empower HR professionals across every operational sphere in Maharashtra.

Domain 01

HR Education, Training & Professional Practice

  • Advance education, applied research, and professional training in HRM, Industrial Relations, Employee Relations, Payroll, Labour Welfare, and allied functions across factories, commercial establishments, and institutions.
  • Conduct workshops, seminars, conclaves, webinars, faculty development programmes, and certification courses in modern people management and workplace governance.
  • Establish, manage, and operate HR learning academies, research centres, knowledge libraries, digital platforms, and incubation centres of excellence.
Pillar: Capacity
Domain 02

Labour Codes & Applied Statutory Compliance

  • Deliver practical training on compliance with central and state labour legislation, including the Code on Wages, Industrial Relations Code, Code on Social Security, and OSH&WC Code.
  • Guide organizations on the Factories Act, Shops & Establishments Act, CLRA, EPF, ESIC, Bonus, Gratuity, Labour Welfare Fund, and laws governing gig/platform workers.
  • Formulate practical compliance manuals, filing calendars, and audit readiness checklists for HR managers.
Pillar: Legal Mastery
Domain 03

Digital HR, Analytics & AI Transformation

  • Promote the adoption of Digital HR, People Analytics, Artificial Intelligence (AI), Machine Learning, HRIS, and automation in workplace processes.
  • Educate HR leaders on ethical AI governance, employee data privacy, cybersecurity, and cloud HR platforms.
  • Bridge traditional administrative HR practices with modern data-driven decision-making.
Pillar: Future of Work
Domain 04

Youth Employability, Apprenticeships & Academic Collaboration

  • Partner with universities, colleges, and management institutes for curriculum alignment, guest lectures, and student mentorship.
  • Facilitate youth employability through national apprenticeship programs (NAPS/NATS), CV clinics, mock interviews, and campus recruitment drives.
  • Support students and job seekers in HR, MSW, MBA, Law, and Commerce with scholarships, internships, and live industrial projects.
Pillar: Next Generation
Domain 05

Industry, MIDC & Institutional Partnerships

  • Collaborate with manufacturing units, MIDC industrial clusters, MSMEs, IT/ITES firms, and healthcare organizations to improve shopfloor productivity and statutory compliance.
  • Partner with employer associations, chambers of commerce, and trade bodies to promote lawful employment standards.
  • Build international exchange and knowledge-sharing partnerships with bodies like the International Labour Organization (ILO).
Pillar: Ecosystem
Domain 06

Workplace Harmony, Legal Guidance & Dispute Resolution

  • Encourage dialogue, mediation, conciliation, and dispute prevention mechanisms to foster lasting industrial peace between management and workers.
  • Provide practical guidance and advisory support on employee rights, statutory benefits, domestic inquiries, and grievance redressal without political intervention.
  • Formulate industry-wide best practices for contract labor governance, ensuring fair wages and safe working conditions.
Pillar: Industrial Peace
Domain 07

Social Welfare, Inclusion, POSH & Green HR

  • Empower women, youth, persons with disabilities, and marginalized sections through workplace inclusion, equal opportunity, and career mentoring.
  • Conduct certified awareness and capacity-building programs for Internal Committees under the POSH Act, 2013.
  • Promote Occupational Health, Safety (OHS), environmental sustainability, Green HR initiatives, employee wellness, and mental health.
  • Work proactively towards the elimination of child labour, forced labour, and workplace discrimination.
  • Provide relief and counselling support to workers and families affected by workplace crises, industrial accidents, or natural emergencies.
Pillar: Human Dignity
Domain 08

Section 8 Governance, Public Benefit & Professional Standards

  • Develop and uphold professional codes of conduct, ethical competency frameworks, and HR quality benchmarks.
  • Institute awards, recognitions, and fellowships celebrating exemplary HR leaders, researchers, and progressive employers across Maharashtra.
  • Operate strictly in accordance with Section 8 regulations, receiving and utilizing all funds, grants, and subscriptions solely for educational, charitable, and public benefit activities without profit or dividend distribution.
Pillar: Integrity
Complete Legal Record

30 Official MoA Charter Clauses

For complete legal transparency and public record, the 30 verbatim clauses from the Forum's Memorandum of Association under Section 8 of the Companies Act, 2013 are published below.

Showing 30 of 30 Clauses
Clause 01

HR Education & Training

Domain: Education

To promote, advance, and develop education, research, training, and professional practice in Human Resource Management, Industrial Relations, Employee Relations, People Management, Talent Management, Payroll Administration, Labour Welfare, Employment Laws, Statutory Compliance, Organizational Development, Performance Management, Compensation & Benefits, Learning & Development, Human Capital Management, and all allied HR functions applicable to factories, commercial establishments, institutions, and organizations.

Clause 02

Conclaves & Capacity Building

Domain: Education

To organize, conduct, establish, and implement training programmes, workshops, seminars, conferences, webinars, conclaves, certification programmes, faculty development programmes, and capacity-building initiatives relating to Human Resource Management, Industrial Relations, Labour Laws, Payroll Administration, Occupational Health & Safety, POSH, Social Security, Apprenticeship, Labour Codes, workplace governance, ESG, future of work, and emerging HR practices.

Clause 03

Institutes & Learning Centres

Domain: Education

To establish, manage, and operate training institutes, academies, learning centres, HR forums, research centres, knowledge centres, libraries, digital learning platforms, HR incubation centres, centres of excellence, and professional development institutions for education, certification, compliance training, skill development, lifelong learning, and HR innovation.

Clause 04

Applied Statutory Compliance

Domain: Compliance

To provide practical and applied training relating to compliance with labour legislations including the Code on Wages, Industrial Relations Code, Code on Social Security, Occupational Safety, Health and Working Conditions Code, Payment of Bonus, Payment of Gratuity, Employees' Provident Fund, Employees' State Insurance, Labour Welfare Fund, Factories, Shops and Establishments, Contract Labour, Building and Other Construction Workers, Equal Remuneration, Maternity Benefit, Apprenticeship, Gig and Platform Workers, and other employment-related laws.

Clause 05

Research & Benchmarking

Domain: Education

To promote research, innovation, policy development, benchmarking studies, surveys, publications, case studies, and professional excellence in Human Resource Management, Industrial Relations, labour markets, workplace governance, employment practices, employee welfare, productivity, industrial harmony, sustainability, and organizational excellence.

Clause 06

Digital HR & AI Transformation

Domain: Digital & AI

To promote Digital HR, HR Analytics, Artificial Intelligence (AI), Machine Learning, Human Resource Information Systems (HRIS), People Analytics, automation, robotics process automation (RPA), cloud HR platforms, cyber security relating to HR data, blockchain applications, digital workplace transformation, responsible AI, AI governance, and emerging technologies for the advancement of modern Human Resource Management and Industrial Relations.

Clause 07

Academic & Institutional Partnerships

Domain: Youth & Academia

To collaborate and partner with schools, colleges, universities, management institutes, law colleges, research institutions, Government departments, statutory authorities, skill development organizations, industry bodies, professional associations, national and international organizations for education, research, curriculum development, faculty development, internships, exchange programmes, and knowledge sharing.

Clause 08

Student Support & Mentorship

Domain: Youth & Academia

To support students and young professionals pursuing education in Human Resources, Industrial Relations, Labour Law, Management, Law, Psychology, Commerce, Economics, Finance, Social Work, and allied disciplines through internships, mentoring, career counselling, scholarships, industrial visits, live projects, placement support, apprenticeships, and professional guidance.

Clause 09

Continuous Professional Development (CPD)

Domain: Education

To promote continuous professional development by creating opportunities for networking, mentoring, knowledge sharing, leadership development, professional ethics, certification, fellowships, competency development, and lifelong learning among HR professionals, IR practitioners, payroll professionals, labour law consultants, compliance officers, trainers, academicians, researchers, entrepreneurs, and self-employed professionals.

Clause 10

Entrepreneurship & Consulting Incubation

Domain: Innovation

To promote entrepreneurship and professional practice in Human Resource Management, Industrial Relations, Labour Law, Payroll Administration, Compliance Management, HR Technology, Training, Consulting, Recruitment, Staffing, Outsourcing, and allied professional services through incubation, mentoring, guidance, and professional networking.

Clause 11

Industrial & MIDC Enterprise Engagement

Domain: MIDC & Industry

To engage and collaborate with factories, industrial establishments, MSMEs, manufacturing units, MIDC industries, IT and ITES companies, service sector organizations, educational institutions, healthcare organizations, hospitality establishments, commercial establishments, startups, cooperatives, construction establishments, logistics organizations, and all employer organizations for HR excellence, workforce development, productivity improvement, and statutory compliance.

Clause 12

Tripartite Dialogue & Cooperation

Domain: MIDC & Industry

To associate and cooperate with employer associations, chambers of commerce, trade bodies, labour unions, worker organizations, cooperative institutions, NGOs, trusts, civil society organizations, alumni associations, professional bodies, employer federations, and industry associations to promote lawful employment practices, industrial harmony, workplace cooperation, and social dialogue.

Clause 13

Legal Literacy & Advisory Services

Domain: IR & Legal

To provide awareness, guidance, counselling, advisory, mentoring, legal awareness, policy advocacy, representation, support services, grievance guidance, and assistance to employers, employees, workers, students, entrepreneurs, and citizens relating to labour laws, employment rights, statutory benefits, workplace practices, grievance redressal mechanisms, career development, HR processes, and progressive labour policies, without engaging in political activities.

Clause 14

Mediation & Industrial Peace

Domain: IR & Peace

To encourage mediation, conciliation, dialogue, dispute prevention, and peaceful dispute resolution for promoting harmonious employer-employee relations, industrial peace, ethical employment practices, lawful workplace governance, and sustainable industrial relations.

Clause 15

Employment Promotion & Job Fairs

Domain: Youth & Jobs

To organize employment promotion initiatives including job fairs, apprenticeship fairs, placement drives, campus recruitment programmes, career guidance programmes, employability enhancement initiatives, startup connect programmes, and employer-employee interaction forums.

Clause 16

Publications & Dissemination

Domain: Knowledge

To publish, print, develop, translate, and disseminate journals, newsletters, magazines, books, manuals, compliance handbooks, research papers, reports, case studies, digital publications, podcasts, videos, online courses, and educational content in print and electronic media relating to Human Resource Management, Labour Laws, Industrial Relations, workplace governance, and professional development.

Clause 17

Public Awareness & Outreach

Domain: Governance

To create awareness among employers, employees, students, and the public through social media, websites, digital platforms, awareness campaigns, posters, exhibitions, publications, outreach programmes, public education initiatives, and digital communication relating to Human Resources, labour welfare, employment laws, workplace ethics, occupational safety, and social security.

Clause 18

Government & Skill Mission Linkages

Domain: MIDC & Industry

To collaborate with the Central Government, State Governments, Labour & Employment Departments, Skill India Mission, NSDC, State Skill Missions, Sector Skill Councils, Public Sector Undertakings, local authorities, CSR foundations, banks, financial institutions, and international organizations for HR development, labour welfare, education, employment, entrepreneurship, and skill development programmes.

Clause 19

Grants, Subscriptions & Fund Utilization

Domain: Governance

To receive grants, donations, subscriptions, sponsorships, CSR contributions, endowments, gifts, and lawful financial assistance from Governments, institutions, companies, foundations, individuals, and other lawful sources and to utilize such funds solely for promoting the objects of the Company in accordance with Section 8 of the Companies Act, 2013, without distribution of any profits or dividends to its members.

Clause 20

Recognition & Awards

Domain: Governance

To institute awards, fellowships, scholarships, recognitions, certifications, honours, rankings, and appreciation programmes for individuals, professionals, students, institutions, factories, establishments, researchers, organizations, and HR leaders demonstrating excellence in Human Resource Management, Industrial Relations, compliance, innovation, leadership, workplace safety, labour welfare, sustainability, and organizational development.

Clause 21

Employability & Livelihood Enhancement

Domain: Youth & Jobs

To promote employability, livelihood enhancement, workforce participation, entrepreneurship, career development, placement assistance, counselling, workforce capacity building, and skill development on a not-for-profit basis, including facilitation of access to Government welfare and social security schemes.

Clause 22

Inclusion, Diversity & Vulnerable Groups

Domain: Welfare & Inclusion

To empower women, youth, persons with disabilities, migrant workers, economically weaker sections, senior citizens, transgender persons, and other vulnerable groups through education, leadership development, workplace inclusion, diversity, equity and inclusion (DEI), equal opportunity, POSH awareness, maternity protection, employability enhancement, and employment support.

Clause 23

Occupational Safety, Health & Green HR

Domain: Welfare & Safety

To promote occupational health, safety, hygiene, sanitation, environmental sustainability, Green HR, dignity of labour, employee well-being, mental health awareness, workplace wellness, gender-sensitive workplaces, and safe working environments through education, awareness, audits, research, and compliance guidance.

Clause 24

Elimination of Unlawful Practices

Domain: Welfare & Ethics

To work towards the prevention and elimination of child labour, bonded labour, forced labour, workplace discrimination, workplace harassment, unsafe employment practices, and all forms of unlawful employment through awareness, education, advocacy, collaboration with competent authorities, promotion of ethical employment practices, and compliance with international labour standards.

Clause 25

Worker Relief & Emergency Assistance

Domain: Welfare & Relief

To provide relief, counselling, rehabilitation support, and guidance to workers, employees, students, and their families affected by industrial accidents, workplace crises, economic hardship, pandemics, natural disasters, or other emergencies through lawful charitable activities.

Clause 26

Commemorative Programs & Conclaves

Domain: IR & Events

To organize and observe professional, national, and international awareness programmes including Labour Day, National Safety Week, World Day for Safety and Health at Work, International HR Day, Constitution Day, HR Conclaves, HR Summits, Industrial Relations Conferences, and other programmes related to employment, labour welfare, workplace safety, skill development, professional excellence, and public awareness.

Clause 27

International Partnerships & ILO Linkages

Domain: Global & ILO

To establish collaboration, affiliation, knowledge-sharing arrangements, exchange programmes, and professional partnerships with the International Labour Organization (ILO), national and international labour institutes, HR associations, employer organizations, worker organizations, trade unions, universities, management institutes, law schools, research organizations, professional bodies, chambers of commerce, CSR institutions, multilateral agencies, and other organizations for education, research, publications, conferences, fellowships, innovation, and professional advancement.

Clause 28

Professional Ethics & Standards

Domain: Governance

To develop, promote, encourage, and facilitate professional standards, ethical practices, competency frameworks, accreditation systems, certifications, quality benchmarks, HR excellence models, best workplace practices, codes of conduct, governance standards, and institutional excellence in Human Resource Management, Industrial Relations, Labour Welfare, Payroll Administration, Compliance Management, and allied professions.

Clause 29

Conducive Public Benefit Activities

Domain: Public Benefit

To undertake any other lawful educational, charitable, scientific, literary, professional, research, social welfare, skill development, employment, labour welfare, industrial relations, human resource management, public policy, innovation, future of work, AI, technology, digital transformation, sustainability, and public benefit activities incidental or conducive to the attainment of the objects of the Company.

Clause 30

Section 8 Strict Compliance

Domain: Statutory MoA

To do all such lawful acts, deeds, matters, and things as are incidental or conducive to the attainment of the above objects and to carry out the activities of the Company strictly in accordance with the provisions of Section 8 of the Companies Act, 2013, and other applicable laws, without any profit motive and without distribution of profits or dividends to its members.

Statutory Safeguards

Core Governance Guarantees

MAHA HR FORUM functions under strict statutory covenants guaranteeing complete financial integrity, non-profit deployment, and non-partisan neutrality.

100% Non-Profit Reinvestment

All revenue derived from membership fees, workshop registrations, donations, and corporate partnerships is reinvested directly into advancing member education, youth apprenticeships, and applied labour research. No dividends or capital returns are ever distributed to members or founders.

Clause 19 & 30 Covenant

Strict Political Neutrality

The Forum maintains absolute autonomy and non-partisan independence. It is strictly prohibited from participating in partisan political campaigns, political party endorsements, lobbying for political outcomes, or engaging in trade union agitations.

Clause 13 Charter Safeguard

Public Transparency & Audits

As a Section 8 licensed institution under the Companies Act, 2013, the Forum maintains rigorous corporate governance standards, mandatory statutory financial audits, and open compliance filings submitted annually to the Ministry of Corporate Affairs.
