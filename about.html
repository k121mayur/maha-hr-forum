About Maha HR Forum The Institutional Platform for Maharashtra’s HR & IR Fraternity

Established under Section 8 of the Companies Act, 2013, MAHA HR FORUM is an autonomous, not-for-profit professional body created to unite, support, and elevate Marathi and Maharashtrian HR professionals across Maharashtra, India, and globally.

Origin & Background

A Historic Void, A Permanent Institutional Foundation

Across Maharashtra’s expansive industrial landscape—from the bustling automotive clusters of Pune, Chakan, and Talegaon, the manufacturing strongholds of Aurangabad (Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar), Nashik, and Kolhapur, to the corporate headquarters of Mumbai and logistics corridors of Nagpur—thousands of dedicated HR and Industrial Relations professionals steward India’s most productive workforce.

These practitioners navigate intricate statutory frameworks, manage shopfloor collective bargaining, resolve workplace conflicts, and drive continuous employee development. However, despite their pivotal contribution to India's economic powerhouse, there was historically no dedicated state-level platform bringing Marathi and Maharashtrian HR professionals together under one common institutional umbrella.

MAHA HR FORUM was conceived and founded by Shivram Thavare to fill this crucial institutional void—creating a permanent, prestigious body committed strictly to professional advancement, statutory literacy, and industrial harmony.

The Forum operates on the fundamental conviction that language and regional identity should never be a barrier to professional excellence—they are our greatest strength. By blending Maharashtra’s rich heritage of industrial harmony with world-class HR benchmarks, continuous learning, and modern digital innovation, the Forum empowers members from the factory plant floor to the corporate boardroom.

Strategic Mandate

The Five-Pillar Mission Framework

A comprehensive, structured roadmap translating our institutional vision into tangible statewide progress for practitioners, industry, and academia.

PILLAR 01

Empowerment Through Education

Provide high-quality, practical learning in HR management, statutory compliance, payroll administration, and industrial relations for professionals, fresh graduates, and factory supervisors.

PILLAR 02

Statutory & Operational Excellence

Cultivate ethical compliance and practical audit readiness across the 4 New Labour Codes, Factory Acts, POSH Act 2013, EPF, ESIC, CLRA, and Occupational Health & Safety guidelines.

PILLAR 03

Technology & Innovation

Enable members to leverage Artificial Intelligence (AI), People Analytics, and modern HRIS platforms to transition seamlessly from administrative HR to data-backed people leadership.

PILLAR 04

Institutional Collaboration

Partner with government labour departments, employer associations, universities, law schools, and international bodies including the International Labour Organization (ILO) for research and benchmarks.

PILLAR 05

Workplace Harmony & Ethics

Foster a culture of mutual respect, proactive bipartite dialogue, dispute prevention, and industrial peace across Maharashtra’s vital manufacturing and service sectors.

Founder President’s Vision
“Maharashtra has always led India’s industrial journey. Behind every productive plant and successful enterprise is the untiring effort of our Human Resource professionals. Maha HR Forum exists to ensure that every practitioner—from shopfloor IR officers in remote MIDC belts to corporate leaders in Mumbai—has a trusted community to learn, lead, and grow together.”

Shivram Thavare is a seasoned HR leader with extensive experience across Maharashtra’s manufacturing, industrial, and corporate sectors. Having closely engaged with the unique challenges faced by HR practitioners on the factory floor, in union negotiations, and in corporate boardrooms, he recognized that our fraternity needed a permanent, legally incorporated platform that could offer applied statutory guidance, peer solidarity, and collective voice.

Under his stewardship, MAHA HR FORUM was incorporated under Section 8 of the Companies Act, 2013 as a strict not-for-profit entity. His vision prioritizes grassroots youth employability, accessible ₹100 student memberships, continuous labour law masterclasses, and an inclusive statewide professional network.

Public Trust & Accountability

Section 8 Governance & Non-Political Charter

A solemn institutional commitment to transparent not-for-profit governance, financial reinvestment, and strict political neutrality.

Section 8 Non-Profit Mandate

Companies Act, 2013 Legal Framework

MAHA HR FORUM is incorporated strictly as a not-for-profit company under Section 8 of the Companies Act, 2013. The company’s sole objective is the promotion of education, training, research, and public welfare in Human Resource Management and Industrial Relations.

Zero Dividend Distribution: All revenue from subscriptions and training is reinvested into programs; no profits are distributed.
Statutory Audits: Maintained under stringent regulatory oversight with audited annual accounts submitted to authorities.
Subsidized Student Access: ₹100 annual student tier guarantees accessible education for freshers and apprentices.

100% Non-Political Charter

Strict Neutrality & Professional Integrity

The Forum functions with absolute political neutrality. As a professional, educational, and statutory body, MAHA HR FORUM is completely independent of partisan politics, political parties, and trade union agitations.

No Political Affiliations: Does not affiliate with or endorse any political party, candidate, or partisan movement.
Peaceful Dialogue & Conciliation: Promotes lawful dispute conciliation and constructive bipartite cooperation.
Exclusive Professional Focus: All activities, publications, and masterclasses serve professional learning and compliance awareness.
